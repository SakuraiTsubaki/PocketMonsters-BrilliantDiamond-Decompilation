# Exhaustive Public-Source Coverage Tracker

This document prevents a seed list of sources from being mistaken for a completed **Brilliant Diamond** survey.

No locally owned retail dump is available. Japanese official material is the comparison baseline; every region/language/version/revision/update/distribution/technical source is tracked independently.

## Completion vocabulary

`Not started → Enumerating → Indexed → Reviewed → Cross-checked → Exhausted`

No family is described as complete before at least `Reviewed`, and technical retail-target claims remain external evidence until independently corroborated.

## Game-data/system families

Every public source for the following must be enumerated: Pokémon/personal data, moves, items, abilities, evolutions, trainers, encounters, Grand Underground/Hideaways, Pokétch, contests, ball capsules/seals, followers, maps/zones, EvScript/event flags, Battle Tower, Ramanas Park, NPCs, shops, text/localization, graphics/models/animations, shaders, UI/fonts, audio, saves, network and HOME.

## Events / archive families

- Project Pokémon Generation VIII BDSP category: current census anchor **14 records** — `Enumerating`.
- official Japanese/regional event pages — `Enumerating`.
- unreleased/replaced/beta distribution research — `Not started`.

## DP/Platinum comparison family

Original Diamond/Pearl and Platinum are comparison sources, not substitutes for BD data. Every “same / changed / absent / restored / Platinum-derived” claim requires direct BDSP evidence plus an identified Gen IV comparison source.

## Regional/language family

Japan, Korea, North America, Canada EN/FR, UK/Ireland, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Belgium, Netherlands, Portugal, Australia/New Zealand, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Latin America and any other official Nintendo/Pokémon regional surface found through selectors/archives are independent work units. Region does not imply separate binary identity.

## Secondary research families

Bulbapedia, Serebii, Pokémon Database, Smogon/Showdown, TCRF, attributable datamines, archive snapshots, old wiki revisions and specialist community research remain discovery/cross-check layers with provenance requirements.
